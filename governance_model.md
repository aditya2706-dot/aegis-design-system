# Governance, Maintenance, and Contribution Model

## Team Structure
A dedicated "Core Design Systems Team" responsible for maintenance, code reviews, and final approvals, acting as the custodians of the system.

## Contribution Workflow
1.  **Proposal:** A product team identifies a missing component or bug and submits a ticket (e.g., Jira/GitHub Issue) with a use case.
2.  **Design Review:** The Core Team evaluates if the request is a "snowflake" (one-off) or a reusable pattern.
3.  **Drafting:** The proposing team or Core Team drafts the Figma component and conceptual code.
4.  **QA & Accessibility Audit:** Strict review against WCAG standards and brand alignment.
5.  **Merge & Publish:** Component is merged into the master library.

## Versioning & Releases
* **Semantic Versioning:** Follows `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH` to communicate the impact of updates (e.g., breaking changes vs. bug fixes).
* **Release Cycle:** Bi-weekly patch releases, quarterly major feature updates. Changes are communicated via an internal changelog newsletter and Slack channel.
